# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps a positive environment:

- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences,
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback,
- focusing on what is best for the community,
- showing empathy toward other community members.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks,
- publishing others' private information without permission,
- sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention,
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
maintainers at **alext@tessl.io**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly, and reporters' privacy will be respected. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject
contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may ban temporarily or permanently any
contributor for behaviors they deem inappropriate.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
